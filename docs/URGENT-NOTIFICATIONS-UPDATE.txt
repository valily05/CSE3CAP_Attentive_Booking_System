Urgent booking notifications

Recovery was replaced with a navbar notification bell. The public recovery and redemption endpoints return 404, and the old recovery page redirects to urgent scheduling. Account activation and email verification remain available.

After an urgent request is submitted, its confirmation is saved in the same browser. The bell shows unread notices, the current request status, and a link to the original confirmation and QR. Status refreshes every 30 seconds while the page is visible. New requests have 30 days of secure guest access. Older requests retain their original access expiry. The QR still contains only the ATT plus four-digit Booking Code.

Notices persist across reloads and browser sessions through local storage. Only the booking reference, status, submission time and private access token are saved; customer name, email and phone are not copied into the notification list. Clearing site data removes saved notices. Browser storage must be enabled. Notices can be dismissed individually; dismissal does not cancel a booking. Guest notices do not transfer between devices. Linked account booking history remains unchanged.
